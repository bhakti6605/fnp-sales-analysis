# fnp-sales-analysis
Excel and SQL sales analysis of fnp gifting orders


## Objective
Analyse 1,000 fnp gifting orders from 2023 to find what drives revenue, when demand peaks, and where the business should focus.

## Dataset
fnp orders dataset: 1,000 orders with customer, product, location, occasion, order date/time and delivery information. 

## Tools
- Excel: Power Query (ETL), Data Model , PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers and timeline





## Key Insights
- Festivals drive the business: Raksha Bandhan, Valentine's Day, Holi and Diwali bring in about 53% of revenue.
- Raksha Bandhan is about 86% of August revenue (Rs 6.3L of Rs 7.4L).
- The other 8 months stay flat at roughly Rs 1L to Rs 1.6L.
- Colours, Soft Toys and Sweets make about 70% of revenue.
- No concentration risk: the top 5 cities make 14% and the top 5 customers 9% of revenue.
- Orders arrive around the clock: Evening leads at 28%, Late Night is 22.5%.

## Recommendations
- Plan stock, delivery capacity and ad spend around the four festival windows.
- Use Anniversary and Birthday campaigns to lift the flat months.
- Keep customer support and delivery planning available 24/7.
